# MahrmTH.com — Education Resource Platform

🔗 **Live:** https://mahrmth.com

A public education platform that brings exam-preparation resources for multiple levels into **one place**, with materials students can **print and practise on paper** — lowering the barrier to study for learners who prefer or need offline access.

## The problem
Students preparing for school, admission, and competitive exams waste hours hunting across scattered sites for past papers and practice material — and much of it isn't in a form they can print and work through offline.

## What it does
A single hub organised into six resource categories:

- 📄 **Past papers** — archived official examination materials
- 🧩 **Topical questions** — practice content organised by subject/topic
- 🎓 **Admission tests** — university entrance exam preparation
- 🏆 **Competitive exams** — standardised-test materials
- 💰 **Scholarships** — financial-aid opportunities
- 💼 **Jobs search** — relevant opportunity listings

Users can download official PDFs and practise on paper, alongside browsing digitally.

## Highlights
- Mobile-responsive, **PWA-style** experience (installable, app-capable metadata)
- SEO-optimised (Open Graph, indexing) for discoverability
- Progressive/dynamic content loading

## Role
Concept, design, build, deployment, and ongoing operation — an independent, end-to-end product shipped to real users.

## Tech
Modern responsive web front-end with dynamic content delivery and PWA capabilities.
